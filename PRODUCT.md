# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One person (the owner) tracking their own tasks across a laptop, desktop and phone. Glances at the list many times a day between other work; wants to capture a task in seconds, and to see everything that is still open no matter when it starts.

## Product Purpose

A personal TODO app: one list on every device (Android, Linux, Windows, macOS, web), synced through Firebase. Optional start time, people, and an Android-only reminder that marks the task Due. Success = the list is trusted, so nothing is kept in your head.

## Brand Personality

Warm, calm, unfussy. Friendly without being cutesy; quiet until something needs attention.

Reference: Things 3 — generous whitespace, soft color, gentle motion, a list that feels calm and considered.

## Anti-references

- The stock Material 3 look (default purple/teal, default component styling)
- Cluttered enterprise tools (Jira): chips, badges and toolbars everywhere
- Cold, sterile minimalism: all-white, hairlines, grey text, no character

## Design Principles

1. The list is the interface: chrome recedes, tasks lead.
2. Capture in one keystroke; editing is one tap away.
3. Warmth comes from color, type and wording, not decoration.
4. Motion explains a change (a task completing, a row arriving) and never decorates.
5. Same app everywhere: adapt the layout to the screen, never the behavior.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast in light and dark; honors system reduced-motion; 48dp touch targets; every control labeled for screen readers; fully keyboard-operable on desktop and web.
